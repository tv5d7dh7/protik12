Who we are

Three Ways In (“Three Ways In”, “we”, “us”) is an independent editorial project that publishes this website and recommends travel books. We are the controller of the personal data described here.

Privacy contact: hello@threewaysin.com.

What we collect

We keep this deliberately small.

  • No accounts, no forms, no payments. You cannot create an account here, and we never ask for a name, address or card details.
  • Hosting logs. Our hosting provider may process standard technical logs (IP address, browser type, pages requested, timestamps) to serve the site and keep it secure.
  • Consent choice. Your cookie decision is stored in your browser’s localStorage under threewaysin_consent_v1.
  • Measurement data — only with consent. If you choose “Accept all”, the Meta pixel may receive the pages you view, the volume button you click, technical identifiers and cookie IDs, and campaign parameters in the URL.

We do not knowingly collect special-category data, and we do not buy or scrape personal data.

The €50 price

The price shown on this site is the collection price for each volume: €50. Amazon sets its own local price, which may differ, and the Amazon listing always shows the live amount at checkout. The Amazon price is the one you actually pay.

Meta pixel

If — and only if — you press Accept all, the page loads the Meta pixel operated by Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd., 4 Grand Canal Square, Grand Canal Harbour, Dublin 2, Ireland (and its affiliates). The pixel is not loaded at all when you choose “Essential only”, close the banner, or have not made a choice.

Once active, the pixel may set cookies and send the following to Meta:

  • the event type — PageView, ViewContent on load, and Lead when you click a “Get Volume” button;
  • the volume clicked, its Amazon ASIN, the collection price (€50) and currency;
  • your IP address, browser and device information, and cookie identifiers.

Meta uses this to measure ad performance and to show ads to people who interacted with this site. Meta acts under its own privacy terms — see the Meta Privacy Policy. You can limit ad personalisation in your Meta ad settings.

Withdrawing consent is immediate: use Cookie settings and choose “Essential only”. We then stop loading the pixel and remove the Meta cookies we can access.

Amazon links

Every “Get Volume” button opens a product page on Amazon.com in a new tab. From that point, Amazon is the controller of your data: payment, delivery, returns and account information are handled under the Amazon Privacy Notice and Amazon’s conditions of sale. This site never sees your payment details.

Affiliate disclosure

As an Amazon Associate, we earn from qualifying purchases. If you click a volume button and buy the book, we may receive a small commission. The price you pay is the same.

Commissions do not influence which volumes we recommend. Prices and availability are set by Amazon and can change at any time. We are not the seller, publisher or manufacturer of these books.

Your rights

Depending on where you live — including under the EU/UK GDPR and California privacy law — you may have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct or delete it;
  • restrict or object to certain processing;
  • receive a portable copy of data you provided;
  • withdraw consent at any time (without affecting processing before withdrawal);
  • lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise any of these, email hello@threewaysin.com. We may need to confirm your identity and will respond within the time required by applicable law.

Sale/sharing. We do not sell personal information for money. Under the CPRA, the Meta pixel may count as “sharing” for cross-context behavioural advertising — choosing “Essential only” opts you out.

International transfers

Meta may process data in the United States and other countries under its own safeguards, including the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and Standard Contractual Clauses. Where our hosting provider processes technical logs outside the EEA, it relies on equivalent transfer safeguards.

Children

This site is aimed at adults planning travel and is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided data here, email us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If our tools or practices change, we will update this page and the “last updated” date. Material changes will be reflected in the cookie banner where relevant.

Contact

Questions, requests or corrections: hello@threewaysin.com.
